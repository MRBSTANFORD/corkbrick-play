# FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**CORKBRICK Unveils Powerful Updates to CORKBRICK Play: Advanced Material Intelligence, Carbon Credit Tracking, and a Glimpse into a Gamified Sustainable Future**

**[City, Country] – June 24, 2026** – CORKBRICK, the pioneer of sustainable, modular building systems, today announced a major update to its digital design laboratory, **CORKBRICK Play**. The latest release introduces advanced material intelligence and real-time sustainability metrics, transforming the way users design, visualize, and quantify the environmental impact of their modular creations.

## A New Era of Sustainable Design

CORKBRICK Play has always empowered users to build dynamic, flexible structures using just seven standard cork blocks—no tools, no glue, no screws required. With the latest update, the platform expands its horizons to incorporate a diverse library of sustainable and natural materials, including Marble, Wood, and Hemp. 

Users can now seamlessly swap materials in their designs, unlocking a deeper understanding of construction dynamics. The system instantly recalculates crucial physical properties based on real-world data—such as total weight, density, and material-specific costs—giving designers, architects, and enthusiasts precise control over their projects.

## Quantifying the Impact: SDG and Carbon Credit Integration

In a major leap forward for eco-conscious design, CORKBRICK Play now features real-time tracking of Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) impact and estimated Carbon Credit revenue. As users build, the platform calculates the volume and density of the chosen materials to estimate the total carbon offset (Tons of CO₂). 

By integrating configurable market prices for Carbon Credits and specific material carbon factors, the software provides a tangible financial estimate of the environmental good generated by each structure. This feature empowers users to literally see the value of sustainable choices, turning abstract environmental concepts into concrete, actionable data.

## The Future: A Gamified Island Paradise in Unity 3D

While today's updates mark a significant milestone, CORKBRICK is already building the future. The company announced that the next iteration of CORKBRICK Play will leap from a clean digital laboratory into a fully immersive, gamified 3D environment powered by Unity 3D. 

Soon, users will find themselves on a stunning, hyper-realistic "Paradise Island." Instead of an empty canvas, builders will source sustainable materials directly from their lush surroundings, learning about eco-friendly construction, material dynamics, and sustainable living in an engaging, interactive world. This upcoming version will fuse the educational power of positive gamification with state-of-the-art 3D graphics, making sustainable architecture more accessible, fun, and impactful than ever before.

## About CORKBRICK

CORKBRICK is redefining the way we interact with our living and working spaces. Through its innovative, modular block system, CORKBRICK enables anyone to create and modify furniture, walls, and structures effortlessly, promoting a sustainable, circular economy. CORKBRICK Play serves as the digital bridge, allowing anyone to experiment, calculate, and share their sustainable solutions before bringing them to life.
